1. Who we are

This website (kumarshiv-star.github.io/koramangala-property/) is operated by the direct owner of an independent house located at 4th Block, Koramangala, Bangalore — 560034, Karnataka, India (referred to as "Owner", "we", or "us").

This is a personal property listing website and not a commercial real estate business. We are the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) for the personal data collected through this website.

For any privacy-related queries, requests, or complaints, contact us directly at: kumarshiv.star@gmail.com — We will respond within 72 hours.

2. What data we collect

When you submit an enquiry through the contact form on this website, we collect the following personal data:

  • Name — to address you appropriately
  • Phone number — to contact you for a site visit or discussion
  • Email address — to send follow-up information if required
  • Buyer type — your self-declared interest category (investor, end-user, developer)
  • Message — any questions or details you voluntarily share

Automatically collected data

We use Google reCAPTCHA v3 on our enquiry form to protect against automated abuse. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, including device and application data, and sends it to Google for analysis. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or any other form of passive data collection beyond reCAPTCHA.

3. Why we collect your data (purpose of processing)

Your personal data is collected solely for the following purpose:

  • To respond to your enquiry about the property at 4th Block, Koramangala, Bangalore
  • To schedule and coordinate a site visit if you are interested
  • To share relevant property documents (floor plan, E-Khata, legal papers) upon request
  • To negotiate and facilitate the sale of the property

We will not use your data for any other purpose — including marketing of other properties, sharing with third-party brokers, or selling to data aggregators. Your data is used only in direct connection with this property transaction.

4. Legal basis for processing (consent)

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, we process your personal data on the basis of your free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent, which you provide by checking the consent checkbox before submitting the enquiry form.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before withdrawal. To withdraw consent or request deletion of your data, email us at kumarshiv.star@gmail.com.

5. How your data is stored and protected

Enquiry form submissions are processed and stored by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form backend service. Formspree forwards your submission to our registered email address and stores a copy in their dashboard. Their data practices are governed by Formspree's Privacy Policy.

We do not operate our own servers or databases. Once we receive your enquiry by email, it is stored in our personal email inbox (Gmail) which is secured by Google's infrastructure and two-factor authentication.

Security measures in place

  • Google reCAPTCHA v3 to prevent automated submissions
  • Honeypot field to block bot form submissions
  • Session-based submission limits to prevent abuse
  • HTTPS encryption on all data in transit (enforced by GitHub Pages)

6. How long we retain your data

We retain your personal data only for as long as it is necessary for the stated purpose — i.e., the sale of the property. Specifically:

  • If the property is sold, we will delete all enquiry data within 30 days of the sale completion
  • If you withdraw your enquiry or request deletion, we will delete your data within 7 business days
  • If the property remains listed, data is retained for a maximum of 12 months from the date of submission

Formspree retains submission data as per their own retention policy. You may contact them directly at formspree.io to request deletion from their systems.

7. Sharing of your data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for commercial purposes. Your data may be accessed by:

  • Formspree — as the form processing service (data processor)
  • Google — reCAPTCHA data, governed by Google's Privacy Policy
  • The Owner — the individual operating this listing, solely for responding to your enquiry

We will not share your data with any real estate brokers, agents, or third parties without your explicit consent.
